What you're getting into

Every wave takes something back

Drops get rarer the deeper you go, so the arsenal you build in wave three is not the one that saves you in wave fifteen. The pressure comes from scarcity, not from cheap shots.

Eight power-ups

Spread cannons, piercing laser, homing missiles, wingman drones, shields, rapid fire, nukes and Overdrive. Every one changes how you hold the screen.

Wrecks detonate

Every kill leaves a hulk burning on a three-second fuse. It chains through packed formations — and it will take your hull too if you linger.

Capital ships

Every fourth wave. A 250-point barrier, curtain fire with one gap that moves away from you, then a second phase at triple health.

Supply line

Stripped down mid-boss? Crates start dropping every five seconds — a full refit, on a countdown, in the middle of the fight.

Training range

Every hostile playable in a sandbox. Live fire, real health, no score. Learn the bomber's mine burst before it learns you.

Steel cover

Indestructible crates block every shot, yours included. In a boss's second phase they start teleporting, on a visible timer.
